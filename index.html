Balanced living requires attention to both lifestyle habits and proper clinical care.

Common Ailments & Solutions

Practical remedies, lifestyle fixes, and guidance for everyday health complaints.

Tension Headaches

Symptoms: Dull aching head pain, tightness across forehead

Often triggered by stress, dehydration, poor screen posture, or eye strain.

Recommended Action:

  • Drink 500ml of fresh water immediately.
  • Take a 10-minute break away from screens (20-20-20 rule).
  • Apply a cool compress to forehead or neck.
  • OTC Option: Acetaminophen or Ibuprofen if persistent.
⚠️ See a doctor if headache is sudden, severe ("thunderclap"), or accompanied by fever.

Acid Reflux & Heartburn

Symptoms: Burning sensation in chest, sour taste in mouth

Occurs when stomach acid flows backward into the esophagus after heavy or spicy meals.

Recommended Action:

  • Avoid lying down for 3 hours after eating.
  • Sip warm water or chamomile tea; avoid caffeine/alcohol.
  • Elevate head during rest.
  • OTC Option: Antacids (Tums, Calcium Carbonate) or H2 Blockers.
⚠️ Seek urgent care if chest pain radiates to the jaw or arm.

Insomnia & Sleep Disturbance

Symptoms: Difficulty falling asleep, waking frequently

Driven by high cortisol/stress, blue light exposure late at night, or irregular sleep hours.

Recommended Action:

  • Shut off digital screens 60 minutes prior to bed.
  • Keep bedroom temperature low (around 18-20°C / 65-68°F).
  • Practice 4-7-8 deep breathing exercises.
  • Avoid heavy meals or caffeine within 6 hours of bedtime.
⚠️ Consult a sleep specialist if insomnia lasts over 3 weeks.

Chronic Low Energy / Fatigue

Symptoms: Brain fog, sluggishness, difficulty concentrating

Frequently linked to mild dehydration, poor diet, deficiency in Iron or Vitamin D, or sedentary habits.

Recommended Action:

  • Take a 15-minute brisk walk in natural sunlight.
  • Ensure baseline daily water intake (2.5L–3L per day).
  • Reduce processed sugar snacks that trigger insulin spikes.
  • Prioritize consistent sleep schedules.
⚠️ Get bloodwork done if fatigue persists despite adequate sleep and diet.

Common Over-The-Counter Medicine Reference

Crucial information on indications, standard dosages, and side effects.

Acetaminophen

Brand names: Tylenol, Panadol
  • Indications: Mild to moderate pain relief, fever reduction.
  • Standard Dosage: 325 mg – 650 mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed (Max 3,000 mg/day).
  • Common Side Effects: Nausea, headache (rare at normal doses).
  • Key Safety Note: Avoid alcohol. Excessive use can cause severe liver damage.

Ibuprofen

Brand names: Advil, Motrin (NSAID)
  • Indications: Muscle aches, joint inflammation, dental pain, fever.
  • Standard Dosage: 200 mg – 400 mg every 4 to 6 hours with food.
  • Common Side Effects: Stomach upset, heartburn, mild dizziness.
  • Key Safety Note: Always take with food or milk to protect stomach lining.

Loratadine

Brand names: Claritin
  • Indications: Seasonal allergies, sneezing, runny nose, watery eyes.
  • Standard Dosage: 10 mg once daily (non-drowsy formulation).
  • Common Side Effects: Dry mouth, fatigue, mild headache.
  • Key Safety Note: Do not exceed 10 mg per 24 hours unless directed by a physician.
